# Two-week trial (v2.2)

Code passing tests is not evidence that Arohexis helps anyone start. This trial is.

## Setup

- **Who:** the owner, plus three to five other people who say they put things off. Nobody is paid and nobody is pushed to use it.
- **How:** on a phone, installed to the Home Screen (iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen). Start fresh, not the demo.
- **First day:** make the one plan the welcome asks for, and add it to your calendar.
- **Then:** use it only when you notice you are avoiding something. Do the weekly check-in when it appears.
- **Length:** two weeks. Write answers on day 14, before looking at Insights.

The app sends nothing anywhere, so these answers are the only data. Ask people to send them by message.

## The five questions

1. **When did you open it?** While you were avoiding something, or only afterwards, or not at all? Roughly how many times each.
2. **Did the calendar alarm bring you back?** Did it go off, did you act on it, or did you dismiss it?
3. **Did the advice go stale?** Did you start skimming it? Was there one that worked better than the rest?
4. **Did "Smaller still" ever turn a "Not now" into a start?** Or did you skip it?
5. **Did the check-in score move?** Compare week 1 and week 2 of "How automatic starting feels", and say whether that matches how it felt.

## What the answers decide

| If most people say… | Then |
|---|---|
| They opened it only afterwards, and the calendar alarm was ignored | Push reminders are worth a server (plan M18 draft): build them |
| The calendar alarm brought them back | Keep the calendar route; no server |
| The advice went stale | Advice needs to know the task: revisit typing a task, or more responses |
| Smaller still was skipped | Remove it |
| Nothing moved and nobody came back | Stop adding features; rethink the entry point |

## Pass or fail

**Pass:** at least three of five open it unprompted between day 3 and day 7. **Fail:** fewer than three. Then stop building and rethink the way back in.
